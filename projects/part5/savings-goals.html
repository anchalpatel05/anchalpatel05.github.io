How to Actually Hit Your Goals

🔄

Automate it

Set up an auto-transfer on payday. Even $20/week = $1,040/year without thinking about it.

🪙

Round-up rule

Every time you spend $7.40, round up to $8 and move $0.60 to savings. It adds up fast.

📦

Separate accounts

Open a free high-yield savings account (try Ally or Marcus) just for goals. Out of sight, out of mind.

🎯

Name your goals

"Spring Break Fund" is more motivating than just "savings." A named goal has a purpose.
