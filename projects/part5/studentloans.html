Types of Student Loans

Always exhaust federal options before considering private loans.

Best Option First

✅

Direct Subsidized Loans

Rate: 5.50%

Undergrads with financial need

  • Gov't pays interest while in school
  • 6-month grace period after graduation
  • Lower interest rates
  • Income-driven repayment options

⚠️

Direct Unsubsidized Loans

Rate: 5.50%

All students regardless of need

  • Interest accrues from day one
  • 6-month grace period after graduation
  • Same repayment options as subsidized
  • Can capitalize unpaid interest

⚡

PLUS Loans (Parent/Grad)

Rate: 8.05%

Parents or grad students

  • Highest federal loan interest rate
  • No grace period
  • Credit check required
  • Borrow up to full cost of attendance

🚫

Private Student Loans

Rate: 4–15%+

Students needing more funds

  • Variable or fixed rates — often higher
  • No income-driven repayment
  • No federal forgiveness programs

Repayment Plans Explained

Federal loans come with flexible options. Know them before you graduate.

10 years

Standard Repayment

Fixed payments. Pay the least interest overall. Best if you can afford the monthly payment.

10 years

Graduated Repayment

Payments start low and increase every 2 years. Good if you expect income to grow.

20–25 years

Income-Based (IBR)

Payments capped at 10–15% of discretionary income. Remaining balance forgiven after term.

20–25 years

SAVE Plan

Newest IDR plan. Payments as low as 5% of discretionary income. Gov't covers unpaid interest.

10 years

Public Service Forgiveness

Work for gov't or non-profit. After 120 qualifying payments, remaining balance forgiven tax-free.

Debt Traps to Avoid

✗

Making only minimum payments on credit cards (interest compounds fast)

✗

Taking out private loans before maxing federal aid options

✗

Borrowing more than your expected first-year salary

✗

Using credit cards to cover everyday expenses you can't pay off monthly

✗

Co-signing loans without understanding the risk

✗

Ignoring your loan servicer emails and missing repayment deadlines
