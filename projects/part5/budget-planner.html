What is a budget?

A budget is simply a plan for how you'll spend your money each month. It tells your money where to go — instead of wondering where it went.

You just need to know: how much comes in and how much goes out.

Quick tip: If income > expenses, that's your savings. If expenses > income, that's a deficit — fix it before it compounds.

Needs vs. Wants

The most important distinction in budgeting. Be honest with yourself.

Needs ✅

  • Rent & utilities
  • Groceries (basic)
  • Bus pass / gas
  • Textbooks (required)
  • Health insurance
  • Medications

Wants ⚡

  • Daily Starbucks
  • Dining out often
  • New clothes (non-essential)
  • Gaming / apps
  • Concerts & events
  • Multiple streaming services

5 Steps to Your First Budget

Thirty minutes. Life-changing results.

01

Know your income

Add every source: part-time job, financial aid, parental help. Use your take-home (after-tax) number.

02

List fixed expenses

Costs that stay the same every month — rent, phone bill, subscriptions, loan payments.

03

Track variable spending

For one month, log every purchase. You'll discover exactly where money leaks without noticing.

04

Apply the 50/30/20 rule

Allocate 50% to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to savings. Adjust to fit your actual life.

05

Review every Sunday

5 minutes to check in. On track? Great. Overspent? Adjust before it snowballs.
