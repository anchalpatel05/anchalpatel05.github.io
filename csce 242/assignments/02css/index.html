Soccer Skills and Positions

Understanding the Game

Soccer is a team sport played between two teams of eleven players with a spherical ball. It is played on a rectangular field with a goal at each end. Players need to communicate effectively and work together to succeed. Every position has an important job during the game.

Players can play different positions all depending on what they are good at, their strengths and weaknesses. Goalkeepers need to be quick and agile, while defenders should be strong and good at tackling. Midfielders need to have good stamina and be able to pass the ball accurately, and forwards should be fast and good at shooting.

The Positions

Position Job Key Skills
Goalkeeper Protect the goal Quick reflexes, agility
Defender Stop opposing players Strength, tackling
Midfielder Control the midfield, connect the offense and defense Stamina, passing
Forward Score goals Speed, shooting
Winger Provide width to the attack, deliver crosses Speed, dribbling
